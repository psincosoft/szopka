# The carols

The 25 kolędy of Szopka, in the disk's order. Wśród nocnej ciszy always begins; the others follow
shuffled, each once before any comes again.

Only music free everywhere: everyone named as its maker (composer or arranger) died before 1926, and
every source is older than 1931. The words are not used.

The notes come from the old prints themselves, never from a modern edition: each melody was read
from the page named below and the reading checked by a second, independent one. Where the print
has a second voice, that is kept as printed. The settings for the band are ours: the chords under
the tune, the fills, the form, and who plays what among the fiddles, the basy, the dudy's drone and
the lira korbowa.

The sources:

- **S** ks. Jan Siedlecki (1829–1902), *Śpiewniczek zawierający pieśni kościelne z melodyami dla
  użytku młodzieży szkolnej*, 5th edition, Kraków 1908. Biblioteka Jagiellońska, in the Jagiellońska
  Biblioteka Cyfrowa.
- **M** ks. Michał Marcin Mioduszewski (1787–1868), *Pastorałki i kolędy z melodyjami*, Kraków 1843.
  Biblioteka Narodowa, in Polona. The book is set in the tenor clef; its tunes are written here an
  octave up.
- **K** Karol Miarka (1825–1882), *Kantyczki*, Mikołów 1904. Biblioteka Narodowa, in Polona.
- **G** *Kolędy polskie: wybór 21 najulubieńszych kolęd na fortepian wraz z tekstem*, Lwów: G. Seyfarth,
  about 1920 (the library's date). Biblioteka Główna Akademii Muzycznej w Gdańsku, in the Pomorska
  Biblioteka Cyfrowa.
- **E** Joseph Ignaz Schnabel's *Transeamus usque Bethlehem*, the chorus parts in Dr. Wilhelm Esser's
  (1862–1901) manuscript copy, Erkelenz 1892. IMSLP.
